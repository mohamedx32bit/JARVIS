# JARVIS — A Personal AI Assistant

**Building an AI assistant that brings intelligence closer to the user.**

JARVIS is a personal AI assistant project that I am developing as part of my journey in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence.

The idea started with a question: *What if I could build an assistant that understands what I need and helps me interact with my computer, instead of simply responding to commands?*

I want to explore how AI can go beyond answering questions and become a practical tool that helps people work, learn, create, and solve problems.

## My Vision

My long-term goal is to build a local-first AI assistant that can:

- **Understand natural language:** Communicate with users through text and voice.
- **Interact with the computer:** Help manage files, applications, and everyday computer tasks.
- **Use memory and context:** Maintain useful context across interactions.
- **Assist with development:** Support programming, problem-solving, and technical tasks.
- **Work locally whenever possible:** Explore running AI models on personal hardware rather than depending entirely on cloud services.

These are long-term goals, and I am developing the project step by step.

## What I Am Working On

I am building the foundations of JARVIS, including:

- Hardware and system resource monitoring.
- Resource analysis and management.
- Project configuration and model selection.
- A modular structure for connecting different components.
- The foundations for future AI, voice, and perception capabilities.

## Building With Limited Resources

One of the most interesting parts of this project is the hardware challenge.

I do not have access to a powerful workstation or expensive equipment. I work with an older computer and an NVIDIA RTX 3050 with 6 GB of VRAM.

This limitation has pushed me to think more carefully about memory usage, computational efficiency, and the possibility of running AI models locally.

Rather than waiting until I have better equipment, I decided to start with what I have and learn by building.

## Why I Am Building It

For me, JARVIS is more than a software project. It is an opportunity to understand how different areas of Computer Science come together: programming, artificial intelligence, machine learning, hardware, and human-computer interaction.

I am particularly interested in technology that can have a positive impact on people's lives. I am still learning, experimenting, and making mistakes, but every part of this project helps me understand more about the field I want to pursue.

I hope to keep improving JARVIS and eventually turn it into something genuinely useful.
